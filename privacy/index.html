Privacy Policy — Ponto Central

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Ponto Central respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information collected through our platform, in accordance with applicable law, including the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13.709/2018).

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, we may process:

  • Basic account information.
  • Authentication information provided through third-party services.
  • TikTok and YouTube account information authorized by the user.
  • Videos and media uploaded for content management and publishing.
  • Technical information necessary for platform operation.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain platform functionality.
  • Authenticate users and connect third-party accounts.
  • Enable authorized video publishing.
  • Generate suggested captions and hashtags for videos.
  • Improve platform security and performance.
  • Provide technical support.

4. Third-Party Services

When users connect their TikTok or YouTube accounts, information may be processed through the TikTok and YouTube APIs according to the permissions explicitly authorized by the user.

To generate suggested captions and hashtags, video transcripts may be processed by an artificial intelligence service provider acting on our behalf.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

We adopt reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse. Access tokens for connected accounts are stored encrypted.

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the services or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. User Rights

Users may request access, correction or deletion of their personal information, as well as exercise the other rights provided by the LGPD, by contacting us at the address below.

Users may also disconnect their accounts in Ponto Central at any time and revoke third-party account permissions through the respective platform.

7. Cookies

The platform may use essential cookies or similar technologies required for authentication, security and functionality.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be published on this page.
